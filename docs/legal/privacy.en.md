# Privacy Policy (draft)

> DRAFT for legal review. Not the final version. Date: 19 Oct 2026 (stage 30).

**What we store**
- Telegram id, name and username, interface language, the purpose you selected, sign-up date and last visit.
- Settings (format, style, voice, delivery), job history (type, status, time, ratings) and processing costs.
- Uploaded videos, photos and voice notes and the finished clips — only while processing and no longer than 7 days, then they are deleted automatically.
- An example reel for “🎯 Edit like an example” (a file or a link) — only to analyse its editing style: it never ends up in your video and is deleted right after the analysis; we keep only the style description (pace, pauses, caption look, punch-ins, sound, colour) and a fingerprint of the file/link so the same example is not analysed twice. Videos from links are downloaded only for this analysis; send examples you are allowed to use as a reference.
- **Links to other people’s videos** (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, etc.): the link itself, its fingerprint (hash), the video’s title and channel reported by the platform (for the “Source” line of the result), and **the record of your rights confirmation** (your Telegram id, the link, the time). The downloaded video is deleted with the other files after 7 days; the confirmation record is kept while the account exists (period to be confirmed by counsel) — it is needed to answer a rights holder’s complaint.
- **Blocked links** (after a rights holder’s complaint): the link’s fingerprint, the job number, the reason and the date — kept indefinitely so the link is never downloaded again.
- **Translation and voice-over**: the speech transcript, the phrase-by-phrase translation and the synthetic narration — in the job folder, deleted after 7 days. Voices of people in the video are never copied or cloned.
- **Beta testers**: a note that access was granted automatically or by @username, and the date.

**Why**
To perform your tasks, count limits and credits, answer requests and improve the service. We do not sell data and do not use your videos for advertising.

**Who receives data (processors)**
- **Anthropic (Claude)** — the task text, speech transcript and frame descriptions for edit plans, scripts and translations (including translations for voice-overs).
- **Google (Gemini API, Cloud Text-to-Speech)** — frames and video fragments for scene understanding and finding moments in games, the example reel for the style analysis (deleted from Google right after the answer), text for narration and translation. On the free Gemini API tier Google may use requests to improve its products.
- **Openverse** — search queries (keywords) for open photos and music; your files are not sent there.
- **Telegram** — delivering messages and files.
- **YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and other platforms** — only a request for the public video page of the link you sent (via yt-dlp, without signing in and without cookies); none of your data is sent there.
- **Google Colab** — only if you upload an avatar job there yourself.

**Your rights**
You can ask the administrator to delete your data and account, turn off bot news in ⚙️ Settings, and stop using the bot at any time.

**Rights holders’ complaints**
On a complaint the administrator deletes the job’s files (the /takedown command) and blocks the link; the log keeps the job number and the link’s fingerprint.

**Security**
Data is stored on the service’s server; only administrators have access. Files are deleted automatically when the retention period ends.

**Changes**
This policy may be updated; the current version is always available in the bot.
